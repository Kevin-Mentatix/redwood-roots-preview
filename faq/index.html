FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything people ask about Redwood Roots — brands, licensing, coverage, and partnerships.

What is Redwood Roots?

Redwood Roots Family is a grassroots licensed cannabis distributor (C11-0000711-LIC) headquartered in Garberville, in Humboldt County's Emerald Triangle. We distribute 17 legacy Humboldt cannabis brands to licensed dispensaries across California.

Where is Redwood Roots located?

Our head office is at 6840 Benbow Dr, Garberville, CA 95542 — on the South Fork Eel River in Southern Humboldt County.

What brands does Redwood Roots distribute?

Seventeen legacy Emerald Triangle brands: Redwood Roots, Ridgeline Farms, Huckleberry Hill Farms, Farm Cut, Emerald Spirit Botanicals, Happyday Farms, Whitethorn Valley Farm, Briceland Forest Farm, Budularo, Savage Farms, Trinitrees Farm, Canna Country Farms, Sticky Fields, Sunroots Farm, Wild Weed Botanicals, Redwood Humboldt Healing, Rebel Grown.

Where can I buy Redwood Roots products?

Our brands are on the shelves of licensed dispensaries across California — see the store locator for the interactive map and full list.

Do you offer home delivery?

Not yet — but our curated subscription box is coming. Sign up for more information to be first in line.

What areas do you serve?

All of California. Our distribution network reaches licensed dispensaries in every region of the state — see where we serve.

Do I need to be 21?

Yes — cannabis products are for adults 21+ (or 18+ with a valid physician's recommendation), purchased through licensed retailers.

Is Redwood Roots licensed?

Yes: California distributor license C11-0000711-LIC and non-storefront retailer license C9-0000364-LIC.

How can my farm work with Redwood Roots?

Register through the For Farms page. We partner with licensed legacy farms in the Emerald Triangle and handle distribution, compliance, and sales.

How can my dispensary carry Redwood Roots brands?

Register through the For Dispensaries page or email retail@redwoodrootsfamily.com — you'll get the current menu and a rep who knows every farm personally.

Do you sell bulk cannabis?

Yes — we source year-round, supplying high-quality wholesale flower to licensed California brands and distributors. Register on the Bulk page.

I'm a budtender — where do I learn your brands?

The budtender hub — brand education, farm stories, and drop news, with an email list built for people behind the counter.

How do I contact Redwood Roots?

Call (707) 923-1236 or email retail@redwoodrootsfamily.com. The head office is in Garberville, CA.

Still curious?

Ask a human — call or email the Garberville office.

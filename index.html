About our company

Redwood Roots honors and preserves the roots of California cannabis

Redwood Roots Family, a leading distributor of California legacy cannabis brands, carries the torch of Humboldt County's agricultural heritage. We partner with over half of the region's licensed farms — thousands of years of collective farming wisdom, passed down through generations.

Our story begins with the back-to-the-land movement, where pioneering families sought a natural lifestyle in Northern California's hills. Their multi-generational dedication to the same land and the same strains yields cannabinoid and terpene profiles you won't find anywhere else.

FAQ

Quick answers

Where does Redwood Roots deliver cannabis?

We deliver across Southern Humboldt County — including Garberville, Redway, Benbow, Miranda, Myers Flat, Phillipsville, and surrounding communities. Enter your address in our online menu to confirm availability.

Where can I buy Redwood Roots brands in stores?

Our 18 legacy Humboldt brands are stocked in 71 licensed dispensaries across California, from Crescent City to Palm Springs. Use the store locator to find the nearest shelf.

Is Redwood Roots licensed?

Yes. We hold California cannabis licenses C11-0000711-LIC (distributor) and C9-0000364-LIC (delivery), headquartered at 6840 Benbow Dr, Garberville, CA.

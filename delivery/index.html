FAQ

Delivery FAQ

Where does Redwood Roots deliver?

Across Southern Humboldt County — including Garberville, Redway, Benbow, Miranda, Myers Flat, Phillipsville, Briceland, Whitethorn, Shelter Cove, Alderpoint, and nearby communities. Delivery zones are set in our live menu: enter your address and it will confirm availability and delivery windows instantly.

How do I order cannabis delivery?

Scroll to the menu on this page (or tap "Shop the menu"), enter your delivery address, add products, and check out. You'll get order updates until your driver arrives.

Do I need to be 21 to order?

Yes — 21+ with valid government ID (or 18+ with a physician's recommendation). Your driver checks ID at the door, every time. It's the law and we don't bend it.

What products can I get delivered?

The same legacy Humboldt lineup we distribute statewide: 100+ products from 18 legacy brands — sungrown flower, prerolls, extracts, and more, all lab-tested and farm-direct.

Is delivery legal in my area?

Yes — Redwood Roots holds California non-storefront retail (delivery) license C9-0000364-LIC. Licensed delivery is legal to private addresses in our service area.

How Cannabis Delivery Works in Southern Humboldt

Ordering licensed cannabis delivery in Garberville, Redway, and greater Southern Humboldt — the process, the rules, and what to expect at the door.

Cannabis delivery in Southern Humboldt is legal, licensed, and simpler than most people expect: order online from a licensed non-storefront retailer, and a vetted driver brings lab-tested product to your private address. Redwood Roots runs delivery from Garberville under California license C9-0000364-LIC.

Here's the whole process, start to finish.

1. Enter your address

Open the delivery menu and type your address. The menu instantly confirms whether you're in the service area and shows live inventory — no guessing, no phone tag. We serve Garberville, Redway, Benbow, Miranda, Shelter Cove, and surrounding communities.

2. Shop the menu

Everything on the menu comes from the same catalog we distribute to dispensaries statewide: sungrown flower, prerolls, and more from 18 legacy Emerald Triangle farms. Every batch is lab-tested and tracked under California's track-and-trace system before it ever reaches a vehicle.

3. Check out and meet your driver

Pay through the menu, get order updates, and meet the driver at your door with a valid government ID. Two rules never bend:

  • 21+ with valid ID (or 18+ with a physician's recommendation)
  • Private addresses only — that's state law for cannabis delivery

Common questions

Can I get delivery to a vacation rental or the Benbow Inn? Yes — lodging counts as a private address. Use the property's street address at checkout.

How long does it take? Depends where you are. Garberville and Redway are minutes from our headquarters; remote areas like Shelter Cove may have scheduled windows. The menu shows current timing when you enter your address.

Is my area covered? The address check in the menu is the source of truth — nearby addresses often qualify even if your town isn't listed. Or call (707) 923-1236 and ask.

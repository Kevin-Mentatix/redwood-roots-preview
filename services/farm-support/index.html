Farm Support

We're proud to work alongside so many of the Emerald Triangle's farms, giving them the tools and support they need to bring a lifetime of craft into dispensaries across the state.

Built by farmers, for farmers

Getting a legacy farm into the licensed market is hard: distribution licensing, Metrc, testing, packaging rules, sales relationships, payment collection. Redwood Roots handles the parts that keep you off your land, so you can do what your family has done for generations — grow world-class cannabis.

  • Statewide sales — our team sells your brand into our active network of dispensary partners.
  • Compliance & logistics — licensed transport, Metrc tracking, testing coordination, and secure storage.
  • Packaging & brand support — compliant packaging that keeps your farm's story front and center.
  • Legacy-fair economics — we exist to keep multi-generational farms farming. Over half the licensed farms in our region already work with us.

Who we work with

We represent 17 legacy brands including Ridgeline Farms, Huckleberry Hill Farms, Briceland Forest Farm, and Rebel Grown. If your farm carries real Emerald Triangle heritage — sungrown craft, regenerative practices, genetics with a story — we want to talk.

FAQ

Farm Support FAQ

How do I get my farm distributed by Redwood Roots?

Fill out the farm registration form on this page (or email retail@redwoodrootsfamily.com). Our team reviews every submission and follows up to discuss fit, volumes, and terms.

What kind of farms does Redwood Roots work with?

Licensed legacy farms in the Emerald Triangle — especially multi-generational, sungrown, and sustainably farmed operations in Humboldt, Mendocino, and Trinity counties.

Do you help with compliance and testing?

Yes. Licensed transport, Metrc track-and-trace, and state-mandated testing coordination are part of our core distribution service.

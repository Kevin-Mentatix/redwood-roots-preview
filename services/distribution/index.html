Services

Cannabis Distribution

Redwood Roots operates a licensed C11 distribution supply chain that moves legacy cannabis from Emerald Triangle farms to dispensary shelves across all of California.

How our distribution works

We built a farm-to-shelf supply chain so Humboldt and the rest of the Emerald Triangle can reach the entire state of California. Product moves from licensed legacy farms through our Garberville distribution hub — where it's tracked, tested, and packaged in full compliance — and out to licensed retailers from Crescent City to San Diego.

  • Statewide reach — our brands sit on shelves in 71 licensed dispensaries across 7 California regions, restocked continuously.
  • Full compliance — Metrc track-and-trace, licensed transport, and state-mandated lab testing on every batch (license C11-0000711-LIC).
  • One consolidated menu — 18 legacy brands and 100+ SKUs available to retail partners in a single order.
  • Farm-direct economics — fewer middlemen means legacy farms keep more of every sale, and retailers get true-source product.

Why dispensaries choose a legacy distributor

"Humboldt-grown" is one of the strongest signals in California cannabis retail. We make it real: every product we distribute traces to a named legacy farm in the Emerald Triangle, with the story and the terpene profile to back it up. That authenticity sells through — and we support it with merchandising materials and staff education.

FAQ

Cannabis Distribution FAQ

What license does Redwood Roots distribute under?

We hold California distributor license C11-0000711-LIC, headquartered in Garberville, Humboldt County. We also hold delivery license C9-0000364-LIC.

What areas of California do you distribute to?

All of California. Our brands are currently stocked in 71 licensed dispensaries across the North Coast, Bay Area, Wine Country, Sacramento Valley, Central Valley, Shasta Cascade, and Southern California.

How many brands and products do you carry?

We distribute 18 legacy Humboldt brands with a combined catalog of 100+ products — flower, extracts, and more, all farm-direct from the Emerald Triangle.

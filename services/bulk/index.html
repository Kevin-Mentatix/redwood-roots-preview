Bulk

We source year-round, creating a steady avenue for high-quality wholesale flower to reach California's brands and distributors when and how they need it.

Bulk sourcing, done right

We source year-round from licensed Emerald Triangle farms, so California's brands and distributors have a steady, verified supply of high-quality wholesale flower. Because we work with over half the licensed farms in our region, we can match your spec — cultivar, grade, volume, and price — from a real source.

  • Sungrown legacy flower in volume, direct from named Humboldt farms.
  • Verified compliance — every lot tracked in Metrc with COAs available.
  • Flexible programs — spot purchases or recurring supply agreements, when and how you need it.
  • White-label friendly — source authentic Emerald Triangle material for your own brand.

FAQ

Bulk FAQ

What bulk cannabis products does Redwood Roots offer?

Primarily bulk sungrown legacy flower from licensed Humboldt farms, sourced year-round. Tell us your spec through the buyer registration form and we'll match it.

Who can buy bulk from Redwood Roots?

Licensed California cannabis businesses — brands, manufacturers, and distributors. We verify licenses before quoting.

Can I source bulk material for a white-label brand?

Yes. Many partners source authentic Emerald Triangle flower through us for their own branded products.

About us

We believe legacy is important

Redwood Roots is a family of farmers, a community steward, and a passionate advocate for sustainable, top-shelf cannabis from Southern Humboldt County.

At Redwood Roots, we're more than just a cannabis distributor. Our roots run deep in the fertile soil of Southern Humboldt County, California, nestled within the legendary Emerald Triangle — the most storied cannabis-growing region on earth.

We take immense pride in representing over half of the licensed farms in our region. These aren't just any farms; they're multi-generational havens of agricultural expertise. Their stories echo back to the back-to-the-land movement, when families became stewards of this land, cultivating cannabis with deep respect for the environment and a dedication to quality passed down through generations.

Despite the challenges of the War on Drugs era, our Humboldt partners kept innovating. Cultivating the same strains on the same land for decades has produced cannabinoid and terpene profiles unmatched anywhere else.

Our mission

Bridge the gap between passionate legacy growers and discerning consumers. By carefully selecting and distributing meticulously cultivated cannabis, we share a taste of Humboldt's finest with the world. When you choose Redwood Roots, you're supporting a legacy of sustainable farming and a vibrant rural community.

Budtender FAQ

What is the budtender hub for?

Product knowledge that helps you sell legacy cannabis: who grows each brand, what makes Emerald Triangle flower different, and news on fresh drops — straight from the distributor.

Sign up with your email above. We send brand education, drop announcements, and news — no spam, just the stuff that helps you behind the counter.

Yes — we provide shelf materials, farm stories, and budtender education for every brand we carry. Ask your rep or email retail@redwoodrootsfamily.com.
